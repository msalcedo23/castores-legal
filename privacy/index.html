Qué datos usa la app

  • Tu CV y tus respuestas de entrevista: se envían a nuestro servidor para generar las preguntas y tu informe, se procesan con un proveedor de inteligencia artificial y se borran automáticamente del servidor a las 24 horas.

  • Compras: las procesa Google Play; solo guardamos el registro para acreditar y no duplicar tus compras. Nunca vemos datos de tu tarjeta.
  • Métricas de uso anónimas (p. ej. "entrevista iniciada") para mejorar la app. Nunca incluyen el contenido de tu CV ni de tus respuestas.

Tú decides qué información incluyes en tu CV y en tus respuestas. No es obligatorio incluir datos sensibles (como salud, origen étnico, opiniones políticas o creencias); si decides hacerlo, es voluntario.

Finalidades

  • Transcribir tu voz a texto cuando usas el dictado.
  • Asociar y acreditar tu saldo de entrevistas y tus compras, y evitar duplicados y fraude.
  • Mejorar la app a partir de métricas de uso anónimas.

Qué NO hacemos

  • No vendemos ni compartimos tus datos con terceros para publicidad.
  • No usamos tu CV ni tus entrevistas para entrenar modelos de IA.
  • No pedimos tu nombre, correo ni teléfono para usar la app.

What data the app uses

  • Your CV and interview answers: sent to our server to generate the questions and your report, processed with an AI provider, and automatically deleted from the server after 24 hours.
  • Your voice when dictating (optional): if you use dictation, the audio is sent to our server and a transcription provider (Deepgram) only to turn it into text, and is discarded immediately — never stored, never used to train models; only the resulting text is kept, and you can edit it before sending.
  • A technical device identifier: no accounts or passwords; your interview balance is tied to it.
  • Purchases: processed by Google Play; we only keep the record needed to credit purchases and prevent duplicates. We never see your card details.
  • Anonymous usage metrics (e.g. "interview started") to improve the app. They never include your CV or answers.

You decide what information goes into your CV and your answers. Including sensitive data (such as health, ethnic origin, political opinions or beliefs) is not required; if you choose to include it, you do so voluntarily.

Purposes

  • Generating the mock interview questions and your report.
  • Transcribing your voice to text when you use dictation.
  • Tying and crediting your interview balance and purchases, and preventing duplicates and fraud.
  • Improving the app using anonymous usage metrics.
  • Handling your inquiries, claims and requests.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties for advertising.
  • We do not use your CV or interviews to train AI models.
  • We do not ask for your name, email or phone to use the app.

Authorization

By using the app and sending your CV, answers or voice, you authorize TUTOS APP S.A.S. to process that data for the purposes described in this policy. You can revoke this authorization at any time by deleting your data or writing to the contact email.

Processors and international transfers

To provide the service we share data only with the providers named above: the AI provider that processes your CV and answers, Deepgram for dictation transcription, and Google Play for purchases. These providers act on our behalf and may process data outside Colombia. By using the app you authorize that international transmission, limited to the purposes of this policy.
